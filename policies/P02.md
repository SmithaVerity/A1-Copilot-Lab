# P02 — Final settlement rule
Version: 2.0 | Approval: approved | Audience: internal

Final settlement means the receiving institution has accepted the funds and the transfer is no longer in the standard cancellation window. A settled international transfer cannot be cancelled through the ordinary cancellation process, even if the customer sent it yesterday or reports an urgent need. A recovery request is a separate process described in P03; it is not a cancellation and its outcome is not guaranteed. Cite P02 for the no-cancellation rule.
