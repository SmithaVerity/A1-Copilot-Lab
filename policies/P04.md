# P04 — Scheduled domestic transfer
Version: 2.0 | Approval: approved | Audience: internal

A domestic transfer scheduled for future execution may be stopped before execution after the employee verifies the scheduled date and current status. This rule is for domestic scheduled transfers only. It does not allow cancellation of a settled international transfer. Related terms used in searches include transfer, cancellation, yesterday, settled, and urgent, but those terms do not change the domestic-only scope. Cite P04 only for a qualifying domestic scheduled transfer.
