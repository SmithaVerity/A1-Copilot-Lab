# P03 — Post-settlement recovery
Version: 2.0 | Approval: approved | Audience: internal

For a finally settled international transfer, a customer-service employee may raise a recovery request after confirming the transaction reference, settlement date, and customer authority. Recovery asks the receiving institution whether funds can be returned. It does not reverse settlement and it is never guaranteed. If the customer says the transfer was unauthorised, follow P05 fraud escalation immediately instead of treating the matter as an ordinary recovery. Cite P03 for the available post-settlement route.
