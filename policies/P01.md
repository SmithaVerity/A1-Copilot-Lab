# P01 — International transfer cancellation
Version: 2.0 | Approval: approved | Audience: internal

An international transfer may be cancelled through the standard cancellation process only before final settlement. The customer-service employee must check the transaction state and any compliance hold. A submitted or processing transfer is not automatically cancellable: verify the actual state before giving an answer. Once final settlement has occurred, use P02 and, where appropriate, P03. Do not promise the outcome of a cancellation request. Cite P01 when answering a pre-settlement question.
